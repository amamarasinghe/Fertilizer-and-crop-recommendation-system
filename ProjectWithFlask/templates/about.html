Why we built this

Serving farmers with practical, AI-powered help.

This project was developed as part of the Final Year Research Project at General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU) with the vision of supporting and uplifting Sri Lankan agriculture through modern technology.

Sri Lanka’s farmers face many challenges—unpredictable weather, soil differences, fertilizer shortages, and tough crop choices. We set out to give a simple, reliable tool that answers these problems.

What the system delivers
  • 🌾 Accurate crop recommendations
  • 🌱 Optimized fertilizer suggestions
  • 📊 Data-driven decision support
  • ⚙️ Simple tools any farmer can use

Our mission

Bridge advanced tech with everyday farming.

AI guidance in farmers’ hands to improve productivity, reduce losses, and support sustainable growth for Sri Lanka.

Built for impact
  • Keep recommendations local and easy to understand.
  • Make the interface usable on low-bandwidth, mobile devices.
  • Use in-memory models for fast responses with no external calls.
  • Stay aligned with sustainable farming practices.
Commitment

Innovation, education, and national development—technology to create a better future for the farming community of Sri Lanka.
